Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CityPulse ("we," "our," or "us") operates the CityPulse mobile application and website (the "Service"). This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, and information collected automatically when you use the Service:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (encrypted) when you register.
  • Profile information: profile photo, bio, and preferences you choose to add.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location, used to show nearby venues, events, and accurate distances — only when you grant permission.
  • Payment information: processed securely through Paystack. We do not store your card details on our servers.
  • Usage data: bookings, orders, chat messages, reviews, and other activity within the app.
  • Device information: device type, operating system, and push notification tokens to deliver notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process bookings, ticket purchases, and food orders
  • To send order updates, ticket confirmations, and account notifications via email and push notifications
  • To show you relevant venues, events, and recommendations based on your location
  • To enable chat and social features between users who connect on the platform
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • To improve and develop new features for the Service

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these circumstances:

  • With vendors/venues: when you make a booking, reservation, or order, relevant details (name, contact, order info) are shared with that vendor to fulfil your request.
  • With service providers: payment processing (Paystack), email delivery (Resend), cloud hosting (Railway), and image storage (Cloudinary) — each bound by their own data protection obligations.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, or legal process.

Data Security

We use industry-standard measures including encrypted passwords, secure HTTPS connections, and rate-limited authentication to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using commercially acceptable means.

Your Choices

  • You can update or delete your profile information at any time from within the app.
  • You can disable location permissions from your device settings — some features may not work as well without it.
  • You can disable push notifications from your device settings.
  • You can request account deletion by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

CityPulse is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at support@city-pulse.live.
